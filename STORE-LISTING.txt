--- DESCRIPTION ---

Control any Roku TV or streaming player on your network with this free desktop remote. No ads, no accounts, no tracking — it only ever talks to the Rokus in your home.

FINDS YOUR ROKU BY ITSELF
Open the app and it searches your network — on every adapter, with a direct scan if your router blocks discovery. It remembers each Roku by serial number, so it keeps working when your router gives the TV a new address. Or add one by IP in seconds.

TURN YOUR TV ON FROM STANDBY
One click sends Power On. If the TV has dropped off the network, the app sends a Wake-on-LAN signal and keeps trying. (For the most reliable wake-up, turn on Fast TV start on your Roku TV — the app shows you how.)

YOUR KEYBOARD IS A REMOTE
Arrow keys move, Enter is OK, Backspace is Back, Space plays and pauses, + and − change the volume. Hold a key to keep scrolling. Press ? to see every shortcut.

TYPE STRAIGHT INTO YOUR TV
Search Netflix, YouTube, Plex or any app by typing on your real keyboard — letters show up on the TV as you type, accents and symbols included.

EVERY BUTTON ON THE REMOTE
D-pad and OK, Back, Home, Options, Search, Play/Pause, Rewind, Fast Forward, Instant Replay, Volume and Mute, Channel Up/Down, TV inputs by the names you gave them, and Find My Remote.

YOUR CHANNELS, ONE CLICK AWAY
All your channels with their real icons, a quick filter, and what's on now.

MINI REMOTE
A compact remote that stays on top of your other windows.

TELLS YOU WHAT'S WRONG — AND HOW TO FIX IT
A live status light shows whether your Roku is connected, asleep or unreachable. If your Roku is blocking remote control (Roku's "Limited" mode), the app shows you exactly which setting to change.

FREE, OPEN SOURCE, PRIVATE
Made ad-free by MoveWeight.com. Report a problem or suggest an idea from inside the app. The source code is on GitHub.
