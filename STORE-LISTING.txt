DESCRIPTION

One remote for Roku, Android TV and Google TV — right from your Windows PC. Connect to TVs on your home network with no account, ads or tracking. Open Remote Control talks directly to your TV; it does not route commands through a cloud service.

ROKU TVS AND PLAYERS

• Find Roku devices automatically or add one by IP.
• Use a tactile, directional remote layout with arrows and OK.
• Open channels, launch apps and switch TV inputs.
• Type into Roku search fields with your PC keyboard.
• See why commands fail, including Roku OS Limited mode.
• Send Power On and Wake-on-LAN requests. For reliable wake from deep standby, enable Fast TV Start on the Roku TV.

ANDROID TV AND GOOGLE TV

• Pair by IP using the short code shown on your TV.
• Control navigation, Back, Home, playback, volume and mute.
• Send wake and standby requests over Android TV Remote Service.
• Keep the pairing identity on this computer for reconnecting after restart.

YOUR NETWORK, YOUR TV

Free and open source. No account, subscriptions, ads, analytics or tracking. Device control stays on your local network.
