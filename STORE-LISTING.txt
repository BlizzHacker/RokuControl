--- DESCRIPTION ---

Control any Roku TV or device on your local network with this beautiful, free desktop remote. No ads, no tracking, no internet required.

FULL REMOTE CONTROL
• D-pad with directional arrows and OK/Select button
• Play/Pause, Rewind, Fast Forward, and Instant Replay
• Volume slider, Mute, Channel Up/Down
• HDMI input switcher — HDMI 1-4, Antenna, AV
• Home, Back, Info, and Search buttons
• Wake and Sleep power buttons
• Find My Remote — triggers your physical remote to beep

APP LAUNCHER
Browse and launch all your installed channels — Netflix, YouTube, Plex, Disney+, Hulu, Prime Video, HBO Max, Spotify, and more. One click to switch apps.

KEYBOARD INPUT
Type directly into search fields on your TV. Perfect for searching movies on Plex, YouTube, Netflix, or any Roku app.

MULTI-DEVICE SUPPORT
Control every Roku in your home. Switch between TVs with a single click. Auto-discovers Roku devices on your network via SSDP — no manual setup needed.

WIDGET MODE
Always-on-top floating mini remote. Keep it hovering over your other windows for quick access.

DARK THEME
Beautiful purple-accented dark UI designed for desktop. Frameless window with drag-to-move.

PRIVATE & OFFLINE
No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Communicates directly with your Roku devices over your local network. Zero internet dependency.
