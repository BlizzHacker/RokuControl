Privacy Policy for Open Remote Control

Last updated: November 2026

Open Remote Control does not collect, store on any server, share, or transmit personal information, usage data, or telemetry.

The app communicates directly with Roku and Android TV / Google TV devices on your local network. Roku control uses ECP on port 8060 and local SSDP discovery. Android TV pairing and control use Android TV Remote Service on the local network (ports 6467 and 6466); the TV displays a code you enter to authorize the app. No commands are sent to an external server. Roku Wake-on-LAN packets, when used, stay on your local network.

Stored only on your computer: TV names, IP addresses, models and Roku MAC addresses; app preferences and usage counters; and the Android TV pairing certificate and private key. The pairing key stays in the app's local application-data folder and is used only to reconnect to paired TVs on your LAN. It is never sent to an external server. To revoke a paired certificate, remove Open Remote Control in the TV's Android TV Remote Service paired-device settings.

Links open your browser or the Microsoft Store only when clicked. A GitHub bug-report link may include the app version, operating system, TV family and model, and a redacted error, but never the TV's IP address, name, serial number, MAC address or pairing key. Nothing is sent unless you submit it on GitHub.

  • No personal information collection
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertisements
  • No account or login required
  • Local network communication with selected Roku and Android TV / Google TV devices only
